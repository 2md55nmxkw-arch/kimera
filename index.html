Kimera

One body, three skins.

01 — Default The model as it is, lit and nothing more. She began as an April Fools’ hoax in 2008 and is a chimera by design, so the two skins that follow are stitched over this same body.

硝子

Mosaic

02 — Mosaic A rose window cut around her head: fine shards for the figure, large ones for the glass behind. Each shard takes one colour from the point under its centre, and the ones tilted toward your cursor catch the light.

素描

Sketch

03 — Sketch Pencil on paper, redrawn eight times a second. Every line is three light passes of an unsteady hand, the shadows are only hinted at, and the construction lines were never erased. The loop closes here: under the lens is the first skin again.
